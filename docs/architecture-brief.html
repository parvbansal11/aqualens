THE ARGUMENTWhy this is not an object-detection problem

A naive submission runs YOLO on sonar JPEGs and draws boxes. That system cannot answer the question NIOT actually asked — separate natural seafloor topology from artificial anomalies — because a single-frame appearance classifier has no way to tell a rock from a cylinder when both are bright blobs with shadows.

Our thesis is that the discriminative signal is not in the pixels of one detection box; it is in the sonar geometry around it. A real man-made target on the seabed is:

  • Persistent — present in every ping window that covers its position.
  • Shadow-consistent — accompanied by an occlusion on the far-range side whose length is geometrically consistent with a raised object.
  • Contextually anomalous — unlike the range-matched local seabed, not merely unlike the image average.
  • Stable — in the same place on the next survey.

Speckle satisfies none of these. Rock fields satisfy the first two and fail the third, producing characteristic high clutter density. Everything in this architecture exists to compute those four pieces of evidence and fuse them in a way a hostile examiner can audit line by line.

FOUNDATIONPICS — the ping-indexed coordinate system

This is the single most important design decision in the system. Every architectural advantage flows from it.

Side-scan sonar imagery is not a photograph. It is a raster whose two axes carry physical meaning: one is sequential pings (along-track, roughly time), the other is acoustic return sample index (across-track, range from nadir). A detector that only knows (x, y) in pixels cannot reason about shadows, cannot associate observations across overlapping windows, and cannot georeference. So every module speaks one internal frame:

ping    = ping_index_start + along_sign · along_idx · ping_stride
range_m = |across_idx − nadir_offset_px| · range_scale_m_per_px
side    = PORT | STARBOARD          (across_idx vs nadir_offset_px)

Two unit vectors fall out of this, and they are what the evidence modules actually consume: u_along, the direction of increasing ping, and u_range, the direction of increasing range away from nadir.

HONESTY MECHANISMCapability is declared per tile, never assumed

We will mostly work with public datasets that are images, not raw recordings. The architecture must not pretend otherwise and must not collapse when metadata is absent. Every tile and detection carries a declared reference level:

LevelAvailablePersistenceShadowGeolocation
L0_PIXEL_ONLYRaster only, nadir not recoverable———
L1_TILE_RELATIVENadir and u_range estimated from the image itself—px, no height—
L2_TRACK_RELATIVE+ ping index, range scale, sequential tilesyesmetricnav-derived
L3_SURVEYED+ validated nav, heading, attitudeyesmetricwith uncertainty
Load-bearing

L1 is why this design works on public data. A NadirEstimator recovers nadir_offset_px and u_range from the raster itself — the water column is a low-return band and the first bottom return is a bright across-track ridge. When its confidence falls below threshold the tile drops to L0 and shadow evidence reports applicable: false, reason: "NADIR_NOT_RECOVERABLE".

When a judge asks "does your shadow module work on this image?", the answer is a field in the record, not an opinion.

There is no hidden state and no in-place mutation. Every invocation produces an immutable run directory carrying a manifest (config hash, git SHA, model IDs + weight hashes, split-assertion results, device, per-stage timings), the tiles, detections and evidence as Parquet, and metrics.json — the only source of any number displayed anywhere in the project.

DIFFERENTIATORSFive, and exactly five

Each is scoped to what is genuinely buildable for the internal version, with the honest limit stated and the test that proves it.

Open-world / unknown anomaly detection

King USP

Answers "does this region look significantly unlike normal seabed?" — never "which class is it?"

Minimum implementation

PatchCore over sonar-domain patch embeddings. Feature extractor behind a swappable protocol: ship first on the fine-tuned detector's own backbone features (sonar-domain, zero training, cannot fail to ship); train a small DINO/SimCLR encoder on a stratified 50–60k DeeperSense subset in parallel and swap it in only if it wins. Memory bank of normal-only patches, greedy k-centre coreset, |M| ≈ 100k × D, fp16 — about 75 MB. Per-patch nearest-neighbour distance → dense anomaly map → connected components.

The part that matters — per-survey thresholding

tau_survey = max(tau_min_global, quantile(scores_of_this_survey, 0.995))

Absolute embedding distances shift across sensors, frequencies and basins — exactly the domain shift GhostNetZero measured between Baltic and Puget Sound. Thresholding at a quantile of the survey's own score distribution means we flag "unlike this seabed", which is both the correct semantics and a hard bound on false-positive burden: at q99.5, at most 0.5 % of patches can ever be proposed.

Enforced constraint

Schema rejects any record where category = UNKNOWN_ARTIFICIAL_ANOMALY_CANDIDATE carries a class confidence. Open-world proposals overlapping a known detection are absorbed into it, not emitted separately. The word "debris" cannot appear on an unclassified anomaly anywhere in the system.

How it is proven

Leave-one-class-out: train the detector on three of four classes, treat the fourth as genuinely unknown, measure whether the subsystem flags it. AUROC / AUPRC at region level plus FPR on held-out natural seabed. A real open-set protocol, and the strongest single result the project can show.

Temporal persistence verification

Evidence

The insight that makes this rigorous: the target is stationary and the vehicle moves. In image space a target appears to drift between overlapping windows, which is why naive trackers need motion models. In PICS the target's (ping, range_m) is constant. Association is therefore not tracking — it is clustering with a fixed gate, which is far more robust.

Minimum implementation

Tile with 50 % along-track overlap so each ping is covered by ≥2 windows. Project candidates to PICS, union-find cluster with gate eps = max(3 m, 0.5 × median diagonal). Per cluster compute n_obs, n_opportunities (windows whose ping and range extent actually cover that location — the denominator that makes the ratio mean something), support_ratio, positional scatter_m, and a Wilson lower bound at 90 %.

Why Wilson, not a raw ratio

1/1 and 8/8 are not the same evidence, and a raw support ratio says they are. The bound shrinks confidence when coverage is thin, and n_opportunities == 1 reports applicable: false outright.

Honest scope

Datasets of independent single images report NO_TEMPORAL_CONTEXT. Ping ordering for the PINGEcosystem corpus must be verified from filenames before the capability is claimed — the reader sets ping_index_start only when it parses successfully, and never guesses.

Acoustic shadow validation

Evidence

An object of height h on the seabed, at slant range R from a sonar at altitude H, occludes the beam over a shadow of length L. Similar triangles give the standard side-scan relation:

Minimum implementation

Establish u_range; extract the shadow search band immediately outboard of the candidate; measure range-matched contrast z, along-track continuity, highlight/shadow ordering, shadow length, and edge linearity.

The mistake to avoid

Background statistics must be range-matched. Intensity falls with range, so comparing a far-range object against a global image mean makes every distant object look like it sits in a shadow. This is the most common way naive shadow implementations silently break.

What we claim, precisely

Shadow evidence separates real raised objects from speckle and texture artefacts. It does not on its own separate man-made from natural — rocks cast shadows too. Its weak man-made signal is edge_linearity, and it is one feature of eight, weighted by fitted data rather than by assertion.

Survey-to-survey change detection

Mission

Object-level geospatial comparison, no pixel registration for v1. Both surveys must reach L2+, otherwise comparison is refused with a stated reason. Hungarian matching on distance, class compatibility and size similarity, gated at max(15 m, 3σ_nav, 0.5 × footprint).

StatusCondition
NEWNew detection, no baseline match, inside baseline coverage
UNCHANGEDMatched within tolerance
MOVEDMatched but displaced beyond the gate, within 3× the gate
NOT_REOBSERVEDBaseline detection inside the new coverage polygon, unmatched
OUT_OF_COVERAGEBaseline detection not covered by the new survey
Non-negotiable

The coverage polygon is not optional. Without it the last two rows collapse into one, and the system reports objects as removed when it simply never looked there. Reporting a recovered ghost net that was never re-surveyed is the kind of error that ends a demo — so the distinction is a first-class output, not a refinement.

Recovery priority engine

Mission

A transparent weighted sum over confidence, persistence, anomaly evidence, footprint, class weight and newness — weights living in a versioned YAML, never in code. Every detection stores the full breakdown (component → weight × value → contribution) plus the config version, and the inspector renders it as a contribution waterfall. Change the YAML, priorities change, and the UI shows exactly why.

Deliberately not built

No trained ecological-risk model. We have no data that would support one, and an unfalsifiable risk score is exactly the kind of claim that collapses under questioning.

Framing

Presented as decision support, in those words, in the UI and the report.

BOUNDARIESModules, and the rule a test enforces

Boundaries are drawn so each module is testable against a fixture with no other module present, and so each of the five USPs lives in exactly one module.

ds_core   ← everything (schemas, enums, provenance)
ds_io · ds_preprocess · ds_perception · ds_evidence · ds_fusion · ds_geo  →  ds_core only
ds_mission    → ds_core, ds_geo
ds_pipeline   → all              ds_api → ds_pipeline, ds_core

tests/test_module_boundaries.py walks the import graph and fails on any violation. Two consequences are worth naming:

  • ds_perception cannot import ds_evidence or ds_geo. The detector cannot quietly consume evidence or position, so the ablations compare like with like.
  • ds_evidence holds no learned weights. Evidence channels cannot overfit, and "detector → +temporal → +shadow" compares the same detector under different evidence rather than differently-trained models — the only version of that ablation that means anything.

The contract that makes it honest, in code

class Evidence(BaseModel):
    applicable: bool
    reason: str | None = None     # REQUIRED when applicable is False
    score: float | None = None    # None iff applicable is False

Every evidence channel subclasses this. It is structurally impossible to emit a score without declaring computability, or to declare inapplicability without a machine-readable reason. There is no sentinel 0.0, no -1, and no silent default anywhere in the schemas.

How a confidence number is actually computed

No hand-tuned fusion weights and no fusion neural network. An L2 logistic regression on eight features — calibrated detector logit, Wilson persistence, log opportunities, shadow score, shadow applicability, context z, clutter density, anomaly score — fitted on the validation split with TP/FP as the label. Nine parameters total. Coefficients are committed with a model card; per-detection log-odds contributions surface in the inspector.

Answers the hardest question

"Where does that 0.87 come from?" — an auditable sum of eight terms with published coefficients, next to a list of which evidence channels were unavailable for that detection.

DATAThe registry is the interface

There is no data/all/. Loaders never take a directory path — they take a registry query. Every sample row carries dataset, mission, site, sensor, frequency, source filename, SHA-256, ping window, annotation type and licence, so training on an anonymous pile of images is structurally impossible.

Capability gates — verified, never assumed

GateIf false
ping_order_recoverablePersistence reports NO_TEMPORAL_CONTEXT
nadir_recoverableTile drops to L0, shadow inapplicable

Each is set by a verification script. No UI surface may claim a capability whose gate is false.

Leakage controls

  • Group-wise splits only — recording, mission, site or instrument. Never random.
  • Assertions run inside the pipeline and land in the run manifest.
  • Perceptual near-duplicate check across splits catches overlapping windows that survived grouping.
  • DeeperSense is eval_eligible = false at registry level — a query cannot pull it into a test split.
  • Held-out domains, not just images: ≥1 sensor and ≥1 frequency band reserved.
Corpus discrepancy — must be stated in any comparison

GhostVision (JMSE 2026) reports model experiments on 3,110 manually annotated images. The PINGEcosystem repository currently holds 6,674. These are not the same corpus. We report our own numbers on our own split and never claim comparability with their published F1 of roughly 0.71–0.73; that figure is cited only as operating-regime context.

EVALUATIONEvery number traces to a saved artifact

Rule zero: the word "accuracy" appears in no headline result. On a task with this class imbalance it is meaningless — a model that detects nothing scores above 99 %.

Provenance chain

pipeline run  →  runs/<run_id>/metrics.json
                       │
       scripts/make_benchmarks.py   ← refuses runs with a failed split assertion
                       │
      apps/workstation/public/benchmarks.json   {run_id, git_sha, generated_at, metrics}
                       │
                  UI reads at runtime

Enforced by test_no_hardcoded_metrics.py, which scans frontend source for numeric literals near metric identifiers and fails the build, and asserts every metric key the UI renders exists in the benchmark artifact. The Model Lab shows run_id and git_sha beside the table — if a judge asks where a number came from, the answer is on screen before they finish asking.

Every row reports P / R / F1 / mAP and false positives per km of trackline — the number an operator actually cares about, since F1 alone never tells them how many boxes they must review. An ablation that raises F1 while raising review burden is shown as exactly that.

Detection metrics are broken out per class, per dataset, per site, per sensor and per frequency. Single aggregates hide domain collapse; the whole GhostNetZero Baltic-to-Puget lesson is that the aggregate looks fine while one domain is broken.

Framed as protocol, not as score

For the king USP, the MUST is that the leave-one-class-out protocol executes across all four folds and reports AUROC, AUPRC and natural-seabed FPR. A specific score is a SHOULD (target mean AUROC ≥ 0.75). We report the actual number whatever it is — and if evidence fusion turns out not to beat the detector alone, that is a publishable finding reported as such, not buried.

The demo path that must genuinely execute

real SSS data → preprocess + quality → real model → detection + confidence
→ evidence (persistence · shadow · context) → unknown-anomaly pathway
→ fusion → geo → FastAPI → frontend overlay → inspector → mission map
→ change detection → priority → report download

DISCIPLINEWhat we will not claim

  • Comparability with GhostVision's F1 (~0.71–0.73) — different corpus, split and classes. Cited as operating-regime context only.
  • GhostNetZero's ~90 % centroid figure as a benchmark — small n, no reserved test set. We cite its domain-shift finding, which is methodologically sound and which our per-survey thresholding is built around.
  • Unknown-anomaly performance on classes the detector was trained on.
  • Any metric from a run with a failed split assertion.
  • implied_height_m as a 3-D reconstruction — it is a range-geometry estimate carrying its assumption list on every value.
  • A synthetic coordinate as sonar-derived, anywhere, ever.
  • Headline "accuracy" on a task this imbalanced.

SCOPEDeferred, and what is blocking phase 1

Blocked — needs a human

The PINGEcosystem dataset is Hugging Face gated: it requires a person to accept the terms and share contact details. This cannot and should not be automated. Once accepted, HF_TOKEN goes in .env.

SubPipe and AI4Shipwrecks are openly downloadable, so phase-1 work starts on those immediately and PINGEcosystem drops in when the token exists.

Also worth deciding early

The dataset licence (CC BY-SA 4.0) and the model licence for the published GhostVision checkpoints are separate questions. Check the second before shipping any weights.

Explicitly deferred

  • Full raw-format decoding across vendors; ground-range correction using real bathymetry
  • Attitude-driven geometric correction — v1 detects and scores attitude artefacts, it does not fix them
  • Full 434k-patch DeeperSense pretraining; PhysDNet-style physics decomposition
  • Cross-line corroboration — strong evidence, needs multi-line surveys we don't have
  • Pixel-level co-registered change detection
  • Core ML export, INT8, on-AUV benchmarking, streaming inference during acquisition
  • Auth, multi-user, audit trail, active-learning loop, typeset PDF
  • Sea-trial validation on Indian-waters data with NIOT
